One photo, three ways to make it 3D

A single photograph of the cast-iron spiral staircase in Vienna's Schönbrunn Palm House, rebuilt as an explorable 3D scene by a coding agent writing Three.js, by world models (including NVIDIA Lyra 2.0 on rented H100s), and by a hybrid of the two. Everything here ran on free tiers and free credits.

The question

Do world models make 3D scenes better than a coding agent alone?

  1. Coding agent: Claude Code looks at the photo and writes the scene procedurally: stair, glasshouse, palms.
  2. World model: the photo goes to Tencent HY-World 2.0 and Apple SHARP on free Hugging Face GPUs, and to NVIDIA Lyra 2.0 on Modal H100s; each returns a 3D Gaussian splat.
  3. Hybrid: the coded staircase replaces the world model's own stair inside its splat, so photographic plants surround real, climbable geometry.

What this suggests

World models win the first look

From the photo's own viewpoint, both splats beat hand-written code on realism, especially the foliage, which is the hardest thing to code.

Generating a video first changes the picture

Single-shot models (SHARP, HY-World 2.0) only know what the camera saw. Lyra generates the unseen views as video first, so its 3D has a believable back side. Code still wins on walking, climbing and editing.

The hybrid is the useful one

Let code own the structure you interact with and the world model own the texture-heavy surroundings. The two needed only a scale fit to line up: SHARP's metric depth matched the coded stair within 8%.

Interior of a 19th-century Victorian palm house conservatory in Vienna. At the centre, a white-painted cast-iron spiral staircase winds anticlockwise around a slender central column up to a circular gallery high under the roof. The stair has pierced filigree risers, scrolled side panels, beaded balusters and a curved handrail, with a gothic-arched iron gate at its base. Above, a tall curved glass roof on cream iron ribs, with scalloped overlapping glass panes and a large diagonal tubular iron beam. Dense tropical planting all around: Mediterranean fan palms, a tall dark conifer, ferns and broadleaf climbers. Bright, hazy daylight through the glass, soft shadows, a gravel path around the stair's base.

Reproduce

  • world-model/run_spaces.py sends the photo to the HY-World 2.0 and SHARP Hugging Face Spaces (a free HF token raises the GPU quota).
  • world-model/ply_to_splat.py converts each .ply to the compact .splat format used here.
  • world-model/cut_stair.py fits each splat's scale to the coded stair and cuts out the world model's own stair for the hybrid.
  • site/coded/js/ holds the coded scene: staircase.js, glasshouse.js, plants.js.
